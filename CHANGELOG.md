# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-05

First tagged release of SeqFidelity.

- Paired DNA sequence models with full-resolution, fixed-sampling,
  interval-aggregation, and learned-routing configurations.
- Configurable encoder-to-decoder connections and attributed convolutional
  baselines.
- Training, prediction, component-weighted evaluation, and matched comparisons.
- Frozen representation readouts, routing interventions, and state diagnostics.
- CPU-first command-line examples and independently constructed synthetic tests.
- Documented input schemas, representation-cache reuse requirements, and MIT
  licensing with retained third-party notices.
